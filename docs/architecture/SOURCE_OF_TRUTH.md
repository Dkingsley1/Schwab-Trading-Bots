Rules:

- Do not hand-edit generated operator docs as the lasting fix. Update the generator, then run `./scripts/ops/opsctl.sh commands-hygiene --apply --json`.
- Decision logs and channel logs are evidence, not control knobs. Change the writer contract in `core/accountability.py` before changing downstream consumers.
- Market, operational, and research labels have different outcome authorities. Never substitute forward price direction for an infrastructure result or an in-sample research score for out-of-sample evidence.
- Broker truth is the account state authority. Sleeve decisions can be confident, but execution should still defer to broker-truth, risk, halt, and storage guard evidence.
- A token-ready flag is not enough after an auth epoch changes. Account, position, paper-truth, and breaker measurements from the prior epoch remain stale until rebuilt, and a long-lived broker client must rebind or recycle before it can certify current truth.
- Collection health and paper-execution health are separate truths. Safety-parking the executor must remain visible without causing the watchdog to restart healthy collectors or allowing the dashboard to report an unqualified green state.
- Resource-admission exit 4 from the baseline and aggressive collection wrappers is a visible deferral with a minimum 60-second retry, not a crash-budget event. The wrappers must rerun their resource guard before spawning workers. A fresh incomplete-collection artifact degrades watchdog health even when its parent is alive; the launcher retains child-recovery ownership.
- A missing SQL writer with its owner's local-storage pause or an active maintenance hold is deliberately deferred, not a restart target. The watchdog retains existing restart debt, labels historical storms as deferred while the hold applies, and never treats that hold as writer readiness or recovery. Global trading halts alone must not prevent the storage writer from recovering.
- Report commands should open the best available artifact and degrade from PDF to HTML or markdown instead of silently doing nothing.
- Codex guardrails should block mixed-domain commits and separate-domain README/docs drift before GitHub publishing.
- A green paper-soak verdict is not a live-release verdict. The resilience control requires all ten production evidence sections for live promotion and never grants live execution authority.
- Control grades, raw economic grades, and candidate-bound promotion evidence are separate truths. No control may rewrite a weak raw result or imply guaranteed future profitability.
- Bot count is not an alpha metric. New bots require a named hierarchy cell, an evidenced capability gap, independent post-cost value, correlation clearance, resource capacity, and human registry admission.
- Collector count is not evidence quality. Every added collector needs a real producer, bounded freshness policy, payload lineage, named evidence domains, and zero live-order or automatic-promotion authority.
- Source count is not an institutional-readiness or alpha score. Prefer a compact strategy-declared set of primary, official, direct-market, and independently corroborating provider families; derived features retain their original source identity, and a public firm's 10,000-source scale is not a local requirement.
- Capability count is not process count or implemented coverage. Logical capabilities must reuse shared snapshots, every current physical collector and organized bot must map deterministically, and unsupported capabilities must remain explicit live-promotion debt rather than silently starting work or receiving readiness credit.
- Sleeve-master structure, current evidence quality, paper coordination readiness, and human live-review readiness are separate truths. The Grand Master may summarize or abstain, but it cannot manufacture evidence, profitability, an order, or a live release.
- Broad observation is not broad execution. Every non-deleted bot may collect, but paper fills require explicit versioned authority, market-signal identity, hierarchy mapping, quality floors, bounded cohort membership, and candidate binding; legacy paper flags never imply authority.
- Lifetime debt, current-day activity, all-candidate research flow, active-stage promotion flow, and active inventory are separate accounting truths. Carried inventory remains visible and reducible but cannot grade a new candidate; a recovery balance can clear only when authoritative active-book accounting and identity-bound post-cost candidate attribution agree.
- Protected or operator-reserved volumes are never implicit archive capacity. Cold archival needs an explicit non-protected route and must fail closed when none exists.
